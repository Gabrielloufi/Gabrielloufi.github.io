About

Models are only half the job

Most of what makes a model useful happens around it: a dataset with a schema someone validates, a preprocessing pipeline that cannot leak, a threshold chosen from the cost of being wrong rather than from a default, and a deployment shape that matches how the business actually consumes predictions. That is the part I like, and the part I build.

Before engineering I spent a decade in psychometrics: an MSc at UFRJ validating Internet Gaming Disorder instruments for Brazilian Portuguese, four years teaching psychometrics and statistics as an assistant professor, and doctoral research at UNIRIO on predicting personality from text — currently on leave. Psychometrics trains a specific instinct: a score is a claim about a latent thing you cannot observe, and the burden of proof is on you. I apply the same suspicion to model metrics.
